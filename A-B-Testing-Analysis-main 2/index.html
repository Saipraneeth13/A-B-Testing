Analysis Methods
1. Data validation: Sample Ratio Mismatch (chi-square goodness of fit) • 2. Primary metric: Two-proportion z-test on conversion rate • 3. Uncertainty: 95% confidence interval + bootstrap (5,000 resamples) • 4. Secondary metric: Welch's t-test on revenue per visitor • 5. Segmentation: Chi-square by device with Bonferroni correction • 6. Power: Approximate observed power at measured effect size
Statistical Framework:
• H0: Conversion rate of B = conversion rate of A • H1: Conversion rate of B ≠ conversion rate of A • Significance level: α = 0.05
